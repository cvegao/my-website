Research themes

From environmental drivers to emergent community responses.

My work asks how stressors combine, propagate through trophic interactions, and ultimately alter the structure, functioning, and stability of aquatic ecosystems.

01 / MULTIPLE STRESSORS

Beyond single-driver effects

Investigating how warming, organic pollutants, and other environmental pressures interact—additively, synergistically, or antagonistically—at the community level.

02 / FOOD-WEB DYNAMICS

Interactions shape outcomes

Using trophic networks to understand how direct effects on populations cascade across consumers, resources, and ecosystem processes.

03 / MECHANISTIC MODELLING

Process-based prediction

Building bioenergetic and dynamical models that make ecological assumptions explicit and generate testable predictions under novel conditions.

How I work

Models as a laboratory for ecological complexity.

I combine ecological theory with computational methods to trace how individual-level constraints become food-web and community-level patterns.

01

Dynamic ecological models

ODE-based simulations of population and trophic dynamics, informed by biological mechanisms and environmental dependencies.

02

Julia scientific software

Reproducible, performant modelling workflows in Julia for simulation, sensitivity analysis, and transparent research software.

03

Empirical–theoretical integration

Connecting model outputs with experimental and observational data to evaluate mechanisms and sharpen ecological inference.
